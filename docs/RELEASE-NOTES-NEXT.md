# DiPlay next release notes

Changes through DiPlay 0.2.18 are documented in [0.2.18 release notes](RELEASE-NOTES-0.2.18.md). Measured checks are in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md). Device acceptance and remaining failure families are tracked in [connection reliability validation](CONNECTION_RELIABILITY.md).

## Connection setup and recovery

- Wired CarPlay starts several seconds sooner on many head units. Until CarPlay starts, the iPhone refuses DiPlay's writes on the USB network pipe. Each refused write waited 2 s, and the iPhone's identification and authentication replies waited with it: about 2 s per step in reports from Android 8.1 to 14 head units ([#634](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/634), [#335](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/335), [#301](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/301)). Until the iPhone takes its first network frame, a frame that cannot go out at once is now dropped after 20 ms, as it was after 2 s before. The report records `NCM bulk-out ready after N dropped frames`. Pending in-car acceptance. [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)
- Wired CarPlay on iOS 26 no longer stays on "Opening CarPlay…" when the iPhone offers a USB configuration with the multiplexer and network interfaces but not CarPlay mode, as on a Proton S70 (ECarX E02) with an iPhone 12 Pro Max ([#634](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/634)). DiPlay now asks for CarPlay mode first, and uses such a configuration only if the iPhone refuses or comes back without CarPlay mode. The report shows `action=request-transition` or `action=reuse-without-apple-ethernet`. Pending in-car acceptance. [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)
- **Auto-confirm USB permission** now finds the system USB dialog. Android reports that dialog to accessibility services as `android.app.Dialog`, not by its activity name, so the service never acted, even on head units that show the standard dialog ([#409](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/409)). It now also checks system dialogs reported that way and confirms only when the text names DiPlay and a USB device, iPhone or iPad. Pending in-car acceptance. [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)
- The wheel-key service no longer stays on "Allowed, starting…" after the car closes DiPlay. Android marks the service as crashed and starts it again only once it sees it off the allowed list. DiPlay removed and re-added it too quickly for Android to notice ([#606](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/606)), and now waits a second in between. Pending in-car acceptance. [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)
- Car hotspots that broadcast a second 5 GHz network named after the hotspot with `_5G` can use it: enter that name in the car hotspot details ([#617](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/617)). DiPlay accepts the name, keeps the car hotspot's security and lets the iPhone choose the channel. Pending in-car acceptance. [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)

## Audio, calls and Siri

- Fix the playback position DiPlay reports to the iPhone for calls, Siri and navigation prompts sent in the 16 kHz audio format. DiPlay counted it at 48 kHz, three times too fast: 14.5 s ahead of the received audio after 20 s of a call in which the driver could not understand the other side, and 7 s ahead for navigation prompts ([#473](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/473), [#536](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/536)). The position now counts at the format's own rate. Music and 48 kHz streams are unchanged. Pending in-car acceptance. [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)

- When the head unit's microphone fails its first read after switching to call mode, DiPlay restarts recording up to three times instead of leaving the call without a microphone. A Seal 05 DM-i (MT6765, Android 9) lost the microphone this way on two of three USB calls ([#647](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/647)). The report logs `Microphone: recording restarted … attempt=N`. Pending in-car acceptance. [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)

- Music no longer underruns every few seconds while the CarPlay screen is in the background. Since 0.2.18, an audio packet the decoder could not take yet waited up to 0.5 s, and a slowed decoder fell further behind: on a DiLink 3.0 the queue grew to 190 packets ([#649](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/649)). Audio now waits at most 0.1 s after draining the decoder once, which still covers the music dropouts fixed in 0.2.18 ([#381](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/381)). Pending in-car acceptance. [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)

## Display

- Dialogs have a background again on Android 9 and older. Since 0.2.15, those versions drew Settings dialogs such as the car hotspot details without one, over the page behind them ([#640](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/640), [#617](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/617)). [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)
- The custom dashboard turn card disappears 5 s after the driver ends navigation. It stayed on the last turn for up to two minutes ([#511](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/511)). After a wireless connection drop, the card is still kept until guidance returns. Pending in-car acceptance. [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)

## Diagnostics

- The `Microphone: stats` line records the loudest sample DiPlay sent, as `peakDbfs=…` or `silent`, so a microphone that delivers silence can be told apart from audio the iPhone discards. Playback stats for calls now reach the report: they were dropped by the filter that removes the iPhone's own log lines ([#647](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/647)). [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)
- Diagnostic reports log the iPhone's now-playing and route-guidance updates only for the first five, then once per hundred with a count. They were more than half of long reports, and reports cut off by GitHub's size limit lost the lines that mattered ([#473](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/473), [#536](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/536), [#606](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/606)). The per-frame USB multiplexer line in logcat stops after 50 frames ([#634](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/634)). [#644](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/pull/644)
